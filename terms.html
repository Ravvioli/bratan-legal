purchase

logging workouts and accessing your records is free. bratan pro is one optional
non-consumable purchase for additional features; there is no subscription or
automatic renewal. the App Store displays the price, processes the payment
and handles refunds. restore using the same Apple store account in settings
or on the premium screen. a refunded or revoked purchase ends the paid access.

records and backups

you control your training records. the app has no training server or account.
keep a manual backup outside the phone for recovery after deletion or loss.
automatic cloud uploads are disabled. old CloudKit copies can still be
restored or deleted. CSV transfers workouts and routines but is not a full
database backup. exports you share are your choice and responsibility.

fitness information

the app logs training and calculates suggestions from entered values. it
does not assess your medical condition or provide diagnosis or treatment.
train within your ability and obtain appropriate advice for your health.
