bratan has no account or access to your training database.
for help, include the app version and describe the problem. do not send
health records or a full database unless you choose to and they are needed.

restore a purchase: settings > restore purchase, using the same Apple account.
recover training: settings > backup & transfer > restore backup.
Health permissions: iOS settings > privacy & security > health > bratan.
delete local records: delete individual sessions/weigh-ins or uninstall the app.
delete old cloud copies: settings > backup & transfer > delete icloud backups.
