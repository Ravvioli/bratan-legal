2. the short version

bratan is an offline workout tracker. there is no bratan account or training
server, no analytics or advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
your training database stays in this app's private storage on your device.
the app does not automatically upload that database to iCloud.

optional connections are Apple Health, App Store purchases, and crash reports
if enabled in a build that supports them. you can also export or share files.
these actions are described below.

3. local records

the app stores workouts, sessions, sets, reps, lifted weights, distances,
durations, notes, routines, weekly plans, personal records, progress statistics,
body-weight readings you enter, training preferences and settings in SQLite.
when you choose to import Apple Health history during onboarding, the dates
and durations of past strength workouts are saved locally for the calendar.
heart-rate samples are read for display and are not saved in the database.

the operating system isolates the app's storage. bratan does not add its own
database encryption beyond the device's file protection. access to an unlocked
device and its file system may permit access to these records.

4. backups and recovery

automatic CloudKit uploads are disabled because the database contains personal
health and fitness records. on iOS, the app excludes its documents and
application-support storage from automatic device backups before opening the
database. a manual copy is needed to recover after uninstalling the app,
losing the phone, or clearing its storage.

"create backup" creates a complete database snapshot in temporary storage and
opens the system share sheet. it includes body-weight readings and any locally
imported Apple Health workout dates. choose a destination you trust. bratan
does not select a cloud destination or upload the file on its own. a file you
choose to save in a cloud service is then handled by that service under its
own terms. temporary files may be reclaimed by the operating system.

existing CloudKit copies from an earlier build are not silently deleted. you
can find, download and restore them, or delete them from "backup & transfer".
this recovery check uses your signed-in iCloud account and contacts Apple;
it does not send new training records. old copies remain in the private
iCloud.app.bratan container until you delete them. they may contain the full
database, backup time, schema version, workout count and an installation
identifier. uninstalling bratan does not delete these old cloud copies.

restoring a copy replaces local records only after confirmation. premium
access is checked with the App Store and is not inherited from a backup.

5. Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and iOS-only. importing past workout days during
onboarding requests read access to workouts, without heart-rate or write
permission. those dates and durations are used to populate your calendar.

when you enable "sync workouts to apple health", bratan requests workout
write/read access and heart-rate read access. it writes the start and end of
finished strength workouts and reads heart-rate samples for the session's
average and peak. it does not send Health data to a bratan server, Sentry,
advertisers or analytics providers. health and fitness records are not
automatically uploaded to iCloud. full manual backups can include imported
workout dates as explained above.

permission is controlled by you in Apple's sheet. you can revoke it in iOS
settings > privacy & security > health > bratan, or in the Health app.
turning workout sync off stops further session reads and writes. calendar
dates imported earlier remain local until you delete the app or replace the
database. workouts already written to Health can be deleted in the Health app.

6. CSV and sharing

"export data (csv)" creates workout-history and routine CSV files in temporary
storage and opens the system share sheet. CSV is a transfer format, not a
complete backup: it does not include all settings, body-weight readings,
notes or other database records. use a full manual backup for lossless recovery.
bratan does not record the destination you select. files you send to another
person or service are under your control and their privacy terms.

7. optional error reports

"send crash reports" is off by default and is available only in builds
configured for it. when you enable it, the Dart Sentry SDK sends application
error diagnostics to Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry). reports contain
an individual report identifier and timestamp, the app version/build,
exception types and code stack locations. exception text, local variables,
user/install identifiers, breadcrumbs, screenshots, request data and arbitrary
contexts are removed. the app does not start Sentry's native crash SDK,
session tracking, replay or performance tracing. native process crashes
are not reported by this feature.

reports do not contain your training records, Apple Health data, database,
CSV files, name or email address. network providers necessarily receive the
connection's IP address; it is not deliberately added to the report payload.
turning reporting off stops accepting new reports immediately and closes the
SDK after any startup/closure operation already in progress. a request already
sent cannot be recalled by the switch.

diagnostics are kept for investigating and resolving application faults and
expire under the configured Sentry retention policy. contact dev@yasno.us
for a privacy or deletion request. there is no account or installation ID to
locate all reports from a person; an individual report can be located if its
report identifier is available. processing may occur outside your country;
Sentry's data-processing terms apply to these transfers.

an error log is also kept locally whether or not remote reporting is enabled.
database error messages are redacted in that log. other error text may contain
input you supplied, so inspect a log before sharing it. you can share or clear
it in about > diagnostics. it leaves the phone only when you share it.

8. the one-time purchase

bratan pro is an optional non-consumable App Store purchase. it is not a
subscription and does not renew. Apple handles pricing, payment, refunds,
verification and restoration under its own terms. the app checks StoreKit
transactions and current purchase access; training data is not included.
bratan does not receive your card details or create a billing account.
a local premium flag allows offline use. a confirmed revoked or refunded
purchase removes the paid access; an unavailable store does not erase a
previously verified offline entitlement. purchase access follows your Apple
store account and can be restored on the premium screen or in settings.

9. your choices and rights

local records are used to provide the tracking features you request. optional
error reporting is based on your consent, which you can withdraw in settings.
we do not sell training data or use it for advertising. if you contact us, we
use the information you provide to respond and keep it as needed to handle
your request and applicable obligations.

where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction,
portability or objection, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data
protection authority. most training records are controlled directly on your
device. use CSV for transferring workouts/routines, or a manual backup for
the complete database. routines and settings can be edited in the app;
finished sessions can be deleted from history or the calendar, and individual
weigh-ins can be deleted in progress. deleting the app removes its local
storage, but does not remove files you exported, old CloudKit copies or
workouts written to Apple Health. delete those copies at their destinations.
send other privacy requests to dev@yasno.us.

10. children and fitness information

bratan is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from them. it is a general fitness log and calculator,
not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. suggested weights and programmes
are based on records and preferences you enter; seek appropriate professional
advice about your own health and ability to train.
